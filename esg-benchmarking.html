Why ESG Benchmarking is Essential for Business Growth

ESG is no longer optional. Companies that fail to benchmark their ESG performance risk compliance failures, reputational damage, and investor pullout. Leading organizations use ESG benchmarking to ensure compliance, drive investment growth, boost financial performance and mitigate risks.

Emissions Benchmarking

  • Compare your Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions against industry peers.
  • Analyze your carbon intensity and sustainability footprint.
  • Identify emission reduction opportunities and align with global climate goals.

Emissions Targets Benchmarking

  • Track SBTi (Science-Based Targets initiative) commitments across your industry.
  • Compare your net-zero and decarbonization goals with competitors.
  • Identify the most ambitious and achievable pathways for emissions reduction.

ESG Reporting Standards & Frameworks Benchmarking

Analyze ESG reports from leading companies and assess alignment with:

  • GRI (Global Reporting Initiative)
  • SASB (Sustainability Accounting Standards Board)
  • TCFD (Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures)
  • ISSB (International Sustainability Standards Board)

Also, identify key topics and material issues covered in reports across your industry.

CSRD Benchmarking

  • Evaluate Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) disclosures across competitors.
  • See companies’ ranking based on compliance and transparency.
  • Assess performance in key CSRD sections and uncover improvement areas.
