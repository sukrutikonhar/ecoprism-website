Revolutionize Your Product & Service Quotations with ecoQuote

Discover an innovative solution designed to seamlessly integrate sustainability metrics into your product and service proposals. By embedding environmental impact data directly into customer quotations, ecoQuote helps businesses, and their clients make informed, eco-friendly decisions with ease.

Why ecoQuote?

In today’s environmentally conscious market, sustainability is not just a priority - it’s a necessity. ecoQuote addresses the critical gaps in sales proposals by solving these challenges:

Lack of Sustainability Metrics

Customers often struggle to assess the environmental benefits of your offerings due to insufficient data.

Time-Consuming Impact Calculations

Manual calculations of emissions, avoided materials, and carbon equivalents slow down processes and limit consistency

Complex Data Communication

Presenting environmental data in an accessible format is a challenge, hindering customers from making confident, sustainable choices.

Address Sustainability Gap in Sales Proposals

Despite growing environmental awareness, many businesses face these challenges in embedding sustainability into their sales processes. ecoQuote is a solution that transforms your proposal process by integrating sustainability metrics directly into your sales workflows.

Seamless CRM/CPQ Integration

Automated Sustainability Calculations

Instantly generate accurate metrics such as carbon equivalents, emissions avoided, and materials saved for every product/service.

Clear Data Visualization

Environmental impact metrics are presented in an easy-to-understand format, ensuring your customers can make informed decisions.
