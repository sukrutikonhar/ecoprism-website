The Challenge

Customers began asking a critical question:

"What's the carbon footprint of this solution?"

The data existed, but it was fragmented across systems and spreadsheets. ABB needed to:

  • Embed credible carbon and material data within CPQ workflows
  • Ensure consistency using GHG Protocol and DEFRA
  • Equip sales teams to communicate sustainability value clearly and confidently

The Solution - ecoQUOTE by ecoPRISM

ecoPRISM partnered with ABB to tailor the ecoQUOTE platform to its global sales ecosystem - connecting sustainability intelligence directly to the point of sale.

Automated Scope 1-3 emissions calculations from lifecycle data

Seamless CRM/CPQ integration (Salesforce, SAP, D365)

Dynamic embedding of emissions & materials data in quotes

Clear visuals for lifetime emissions, avoided emissions & material savings

Result: Complex environmental data became actionable customer insight.

Looking Ahead

Building on its early success, ABB is extending ecoQUOTE across customer portals - creating a unified sustainability intelligence ecosystem with real-time emissions tracking and interactive dashboards.
