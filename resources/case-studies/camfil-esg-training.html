Building ESG Fluency Across a Global IT Team

How ecoPRISM designed and delivered a tailored sustainability training programme for Camfil's IT employees, turning climate and ESG concepts into practical, everyday understanding.

Client

Camfil

Audience

IT employees, EMEA

Format

Virtual sessions

Sessions delivered

3 modules · 2 rounds each

Topics

ESG · Climate · Carbon · Reporting · Ratings

The Challenge

"How do we make ESG concepts relevant and actionable for our IT team?"

Sustainability knowledge shouldn't stop at the sustainability team

Camfil, a global leader in air filtration and clean air solutions, recognised that meaningful sustainability progress requires more than policy commitments at the top. For ESG to become embedded in day-to-day decisions, the people closest to operations, systems, and procurement need to understand it too.

Build baseline ESG and climate literacy across a non-specialist audience

Make carbon accounting and Scope 1, 2 & 3 emissions tangible for IT professionals

Familiarise the team with ESG reporting frameworks and rating systems relevant to Camfil

Deliver engaging, interactive sessions that drive genuine knowledge retention

The Solution

Three modules. Practical, engaging, built for IT professionals.

ecoPRISM designed a three-module virtual training programme tailored specifically for Camfil's IT team, delivered in two rounds to maximise reach. Each session combined structured learning with real-world case studies, hands-on activities, and quizzes to embed understanding.

What This Delivers

A practical ESG capability baseline across IT teams, with stronger day-to-day decision making and higher readiness for reporting and climate action priorities.

Module 01

Sustainability and Climate Change

  • Sustainability, CSR and ESG fundamentals
  • Climate change causes and impacts
  • IT and sustainability challenges

  • International climate action

Module 02

Carbon Accounting and LCA

  • GHG inventory and Scope 1, 2 & 3 emissions
  • Decarbonisation levers and MACC
  • Life-cycle assessment methodology
  • Hands-on activity: categorising emissions
  • Dell LCA case study

Module 03

ESG Reporting and Ratings

  • ESG reporting regulations and standards
  • Frameworks: GRI, ESRS, SASB, TCFD
  • ESG ratings: MSCI, DJSI, CDP
  • Walk-through of a live ESG report
  • Business impact of ESG ratings

Looking Ahead

Capability that compounds

The training equipped Camfil's IT team with a shared sustainability vocabulary and the context to contribute meaningfully to the organisation's ESG goals. Each participant left with comprehensive training materials, curated self-learning resources, and a clear understanding of how their role connects to Camfil's broader sustainability commitments.

Building internal capability at this level creates a multiplier effect. Teams that understand ESG ask better questions, make more informed decisions, and become active contributors to the organisation's sustainability performance rather than passive observers of it.
