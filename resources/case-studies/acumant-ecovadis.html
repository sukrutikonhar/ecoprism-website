Customer Story

Building the Foundation for EcoVadis Excellence

How ecoPRISM is guiding Acumant through a comprehensive EcoVadis Sustainability Assessment, from gap analysis to submission-ready ESG documentation.

Engagement in progress · Q1 2026

Client

Acumant AB

Headquarters

Solna, Stockholm

Framework

EcoVadis

Themes covered

Environment · Labour · Ethics · Procurement

The Challenge

"What are our sustainability gaps, and how do we close them before submitting to EcoVadis?"

Turning sustainability intent into auditable proof

Acumant, a Sweden-based technology firm, faced a challenge familiar to many growing organisations: supplier and procurement partners were increasingly asking for verified sustainability credentials. EcoVadis offered the recognised standard, but translating internal practices into a structured, evidence-backed submission was not straightforward.

Identify gaps across policies, controls, and documentation against EcoVadis requirements

Develop ESG policies spanning Environment, Labour, Ethics, and Sustainable Procurement

Quantify Scope 1, 2 & 3 emissions and environmental indicators with limited primary data

Prepare a submission-ready questionnaire backed by a consolidated evidence repository

The Solution

End-to-end EcoVadis readiness, from diagnosis to submission

ecoPRISM embedded alongside Acumant's team to build everything needed for a credible, high-quality EcoVadis submission and the internal foundations to keep improving year on year.

What This Delivers

A faster route from gap discovery to a submission-ready EcoVadis package, with clear evidence and improvement priorities built in.

Book a consultation

Gap analysis and prioritisation

A structured review of Acumant's existing policies and documentation mapped against EcoVadis requirements, with clear priority actions identified across all four themes.

Policy and ESG data development

Drafting ESG policies, calculating GHG emissions across all three scopes, and building a validated evidence repository so every questionnaire response is grounded in data.

Submission and improvement roadmap

Full questionnaire completion support, platform submission, and a post-score improvement roadmap to strengthen Acumant's rating in future assessment cycles.

Looking Ahead

A score is the start, not the finish

Beyond the initial assessment, ecoPRISM's engagement is designed to leave Acumant with lasting infrastructure: documented policies, a live evidence repository, an emissions baseline, and a public sustainability presence so every future EcoVadis cycle starts from a stronger position.
