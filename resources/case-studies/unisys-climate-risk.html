The Challenge

"What are our climate-related financial risks and how do we disclose them?"

Complex data.
Regulatory deadline.
No clear path.

California's SB 261 required Unisys to prepare a TCFD/ISSB-aligned climate risk report. The data existed, but it was fragmented across systems, unquantified, and disconnected from the business.

Translating scattered data into a coherent, audit-ready disclosure while embedding it into enterprise risk management required both deep climate science and strategic business acumen.

Unisys needed to:

Assess physical risks - floods, wildfires, heatwaves, cyclones, drought, and earthquakes - across global operations

Evaluate transition risks including regulatory shifts, carbon pricing, and client procurement changes

Quantify financial exposure linked to climate hazards across revenue, costs, and asset values

Integrate findings into enterprise risk management and long-term strategy

Deliver a publication-ready TCFD disclosure aligned with SB 261 requirements

Physical Risk Mapping

High-resolution geospatial hazard data across all global sites under low-emissions (SSP1-2.6) and high-emissions (SSP5-8.5) scenarios for 2030 and 2050.

Transition Risk Analysis

Policy, regulatory, carbon pricing, technology, market, and reputational risks modelled under NGFS Net Zero 2050 and Delayed Transition scenarios.

Financial Impact Quantification

Climate hazards linked to revenue exposure, operational cost impacts, and asset value implications — making risk tangible for finance and leadership teams.

ERM Integration

All climate risks mapped to Unisys's existing Enterprise Risk Register, with residual risk scores calculated after evaluating existing controls.

Adaptation Roadmaps

Tailored adaptation and mitigation roadmaps developed for business-critical sites, giving operational teams clear next steps.

Publication-Ready Disclosure

TCFD report covering all four pillars — governance, strategy, risk management, and metrics — aligned with SB 261 and ready for regulators and the boardroom.
