A Guide to ESRS Aligned Sustainability Reporting

Are you a company preparing ESRS report to fulfill the Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) requirements? If so, our comprehensive guide is designed specially for you to navigate these complex reporting requirements. Our guide is offering you industry best practices of ESRS reporting to help you master ESRS compliance with confidence and ease.

In an era where corporate transparency and accountability are paramount, the European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS) have emerged as critical tools for ensuring comprehensive sustainability reporting. At the heart of ESRS is the concept of double materiality assessment, a cornerstone of sustainability reporting. Understanding and implementing this principle is essential for accurate and meaningful disclosures. ESRS requires detailed disclosures on environmental, social, and governance (ESG) criteria, driving companies to enhance their transparency and responsibility. However, navigating the complexities of ESRS compliance can be challenging.

This white paper provides a comprehensive guide to navigating the complexities of ESRS compliance, offering an exemplary practice from 15 ESRS-aligned reports to ensure thorough coverage of all mandatory sections. This guide gives you a strategy to avoid gaps and offers sample tables, graphs, and charts for reference to enhance the practical application of ESRS guidelines, ensuring relevance and legal compliance. By following these guidelines and leveraging industry best practices, companies can achieve thorough ESRS compliance, enhancing transparency and building stakeholder trust.

Key elements covered in this guide include:

  • Double Materiality Assessment
  • General Disclosures
  • Topical Standards
  • Alignment with Other Standards
  • Other Key Elements in a CSRD Report
  • Entity-Specific and Sector-Specific Disclosures
