Decoding CSRD: Addressing FAQs for Sustainable Business Practices

Does your business operate within the EU?
You might be one of the ~50,000 companies required to comply with the European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS).

Navigating the intricacies of the Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) can be challenging. Are you faced with the daunting task of interpreting and complying with the specific reporting requirements outlined in the CSRD?

The European Union (EU) continues its efforts to harmonize climate goals, sustainability targets, and human rights; it has introduced more specific disclosure requirements for businesses. These requirements set a higher standard for sustainability reporting, with the Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) taking the lead. The CSRD amplifies and replaces existing non-financial reporting requirements while aligning with the Sustainable Finance Disclosure Regulation (SFDR) and EU Taxonomy requirements.
