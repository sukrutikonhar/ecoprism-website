From Reports to Customer Touchpoints: Making Sustainability Visible in Every Interaction

  • 05 November 2025
  • By Navaneeth Rajendran
  • 4-minute read

The New Question Driving Business Conversations

A few years back, sustainability reports were annual compliance exercises and backward-looking documents created for regulators and investors. The next time your customer requests a quote, they won't just ask about price or lead time. They'll ask:

"What's the carbon cost of this order?"

If your team needs a sustainability report to find that answer - you've already lost the conversation.

In today's market, sustainability isn't paperwork; it's persuasion. And the most forward-looking companies aren't waiting for annual reports - they're making sustainability visible in real time.

Recent research shows how rapidly sustainability is becoming a key business differentiator. According to Bain & Company's Visionary CEO's Guide to Sustainability (2024), sustainability has become one of the top three purchasing criteria for corporate buyers. In fact, 36% say they would switch suppliers that fail to meet sustainability expectations in 2024, and nearly 60% plan to do so within the next three years. EY's State of Sustainability 2024 report highlights a similar trend: 64% of organizations consider sustainability to be "essential" to their customer, a sharp rise from 54% in 2022.

Across procurement processes from RFPs to supplier assessments, quantified sustainability data, particularly product and supply chain carbon impacts, is becoming a standard requirement. For businesses, that means sustainability information can no longer reside in a report. It needs to live where the decisions are made, i.e., in quotations, proposals, order confirmations, and customer dashboards.

The Shift: From Compliance to Conversation

As this demand grows, leading companies are rethinking how sustainability data flows through their value chain. Traditionally, in organizations, sustainability data was kept in separate places, tracked for compliance or internal goals, and rarely shared with customers in real time. But as climate expectations evolve, leading organizations are starting to see sustainability as a conversation instead of a checkbox.

Companies are trying to embed verified environmental data directly into customer-facing systems. Imagine a sales proposal that shows a product or service's carbon footprint next to its price, or a portal where customers can track the emissions saved through preventive maintenance, or customers can understand how many trees would be needed to sequester the amount of emissions generated by the product/service. This helps the company to enable every sale, procurement, or service interaction with the customer as a moment of transparent communication, improving their relationships with customers by providing them with valuable insights.

The Opportunity: Turning Data into Decisions

Most companies already have sustainability data that customers are asking for, but it's often fragmented across multiple systems, tools, and internal spreadsheets. This includes critical data on procurement emissions and supplier GHG impact, making it difficult to quantify the carbon footprint of quotes or proposals. When customers request emissions information, sales teams often rely on manual calculations, using Excel or delayed inputs from the sustainability team, slowing down responses. In competitive business environments where decisions happen in days, these delays cost deals.

ecoQUOTE - a solution from ecoPRISM closes this gap by integrating directly with core business systems like CRM, CPQ, ERP, BMS etc., to unify the fragmented data. Using verified emission factors from credible databases, the platform calculates environmental impacts and embeds them automatically into customer-facing documents. This ensures every proposal, order, and customer report now includes real-time, consistent, and auditable environmental insights ready when customers ask for them. In a world where transparency drives trust and delays mean losing deals, this integration turns sustainability data into a competitive advantage.

In Action: ABB Brings Sustainability to the Sales Frontline

Global technology leader ABB Electrification Services faced this challenge: customers were increasingly asking for the carbon footprint of solutions during quotations, negotiations, and tenders. The data existed somewhere in ABB's systems, but it wasn't accessible where it mattered most during conversations with customers. ABB needed this sustainability data to flow through their sales process the exact way pricing and delivery timelines did - automatically, instantly and consistently.

ecoPRISM's solution - ecoQUOTE, a sustainability intelligence layer, enabled ABB to successfully integrate real-time carbon and materials data directly into its Configure, Price, Quote (CPQ) workflows. Now, in every proposal for a product or service, sustainability metrics like lifetime emissions, avoided emissions, and material savings are calculated automatically and provided, alongside pricing and performance data. Within just the first 10 months of implementation, the results were remarkable:

  • 2,200+ customer proposals delivered with embedded carbon data
  • Over 8.7 million tonnes CO2e avoided emissions was quantified

The impact extended beyond the data. This gave sales teams confidence to answer questions from customers on environmental performance with credibility, and procurement teams began citing ABB's transparency as a differentiator in vendor selection. Hence, for ABB, sustainability moved from a reporting obligation to a sales differentiator, giving customers instant insight into the impact of their choices and helping ABB's teams lead with data-driven transparency.

Scaling Transparency Across Touchpoints

Embedding sustainability data into proposals is just the beginning. The real impact comes when transparency extends across every stage of the customer relationship, turning sustainability from a one-time disclosure into an ongoing dialogue. Using ecoQUOTE, organizations can provide transparency across the entire customer journey:

  • Quotations/Order confirmations: Showcase the environmental impact of the purchase.
  • Customer dashboards: Track the cumulative lifetime emissions, avoided emissions, materials saved, and progress toward goals.
  • Supplier interfaces: Share impact metrics across the value chain.
  • Investor & Stakeholder Reports: Use the verified data that powers customer proposals to inform ESG/sustainability reporting, ensuring consistent information is provided to all stakeholders.

Sustainability shouldn't be visible only at the point of sale. It should flow through every interaction - from quote to delivery to renewal, thus helping to reinforce trust, accountability, and shared progress at every step. This visibility enables tracking of procurement emissions and supplier GHG impact, helping organizations meet Scope 3 disclosure requirements.

The Takeaway: Sustainability, Built In

Sustainability is currently evolving from an annual report to a living, breathing part of how businesses operate. With ecoQUOTE, sustainability data becomes accessible, actionable, and visible in every customer touchpoint. It's not just about compliance anymore. It's about clarity, credibility, and connection.

Because sustainability shouldn't wait for a report. It should live in every quote, order, and customer interaction.
