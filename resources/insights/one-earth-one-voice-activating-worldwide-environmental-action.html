One Earth, One Voice: Activating Worldwide Environmental Action

  • 05 Jun 2023
  • By Valli Tirounavoucarassou
  • 5-minute read

Inspiring organisations in the fight against plastic pollution

Plastic pollution has emerged as one of the most important environmental challenges of our time. Plastic garbage is wreaking damage on the earth and harming many species, from seas and rivers to landfills and even our own bodies. On this World Environment Day, let us support every organisation, group and individual who is working towards preventing plastic pollution. Here are some organisations working towards making the world a better place.

Five companies that are battling plastic pollution

Ocean cleanup : Harnessing innovation to tackle marine pollution

The Ocean Cleanup is an innovative organisation dedicated to tackling plastic garbage in the oceans. It is a multidisciplinary group of scientists, engineers, and marine professionals with the goal of removing 90% of floating ocean plastic by 2040. In 2013, Boyan Slat, a young Dutch entrepreneur, created The Ocean Cleanup with the purpose of inventing and applying advanced technologies to clean up the massive volumes of plastic rubbish that accumulate in our oceans. One of their key projects is called System 001, also known as Wilson. This method involves the use of a large-scale floating barrier to passively collect plastic debris from the surface of the ocean and tested in the Great Pacific Garbage Patch. Organization also emphasises the need of prevention, supporting good waste management practises, recycling, and increasing awareness about plastic contamination. Their mission is to improve the quality of our oceans, conserve marine life, and motivate global action towards a cleaner, more sustainable future.

Follow this link to learn more about them: The Ocean Cleanup

Plastic Change : Empowering change for a plastic-free future

Plastic Change is a prominent Danish organisation dedicated to combating plastic pollution and establishing a plastic-free future. They use a holistic strategy to address the issue and effect good change. The organization's mission is to increase awareness about the environmental and health consequences of plastic pollution via research, education, and activism. They perform scientific research, collect data, and provide useful insights to aid in evidence-based decision-making and policy changes. Plastic Change actively involves stakeholders from multiple sectors, including governments, corporations, and the public, to promote sustainable practises and minimise plastic waste. They work with industry leaders to develop new solutions and assist the transition to a circular economy. Education and awareness are critical components of Plastic Change's initiatives. They organise campaigns, workshops, and events to educate the public about plastic pollution, encouraging individuals to make conscious choices and adopt sustainable behaviours. Plastic Change strives to create a global movement against plastic pollution through their international collaborations and partnerships. They actively participate in international conferences, forums, and initiatives to drive the agenda for change on a global scale. Their work is instrumental in shaping a more sustainable future where plastic waste is minimized and ecosystems are protected.

Follow this link to learn more about them: The Plastic Change

Repurpose Global : Empowering local innovators for environmental impact

Repurpose Global is a remarkable organization at the forefront of the fight against plastic pollution. Their principal goal is to help local businesses in developing countries find long-term solutions to environmental issues caused by plastic pollution. They assist these innovators in developing and implementing effective solutions by providing resources, mentoring, and access to a global network. One important way they accomplish this by creating a platform that links these innovators with resources, knowledge, and financing possibilities. Organization ensures that local solutions get the support they need by using their global network and connections. This method not only speeds the development and implementation of sustainable solutions, but it also allows local enterprises and communities to play an active part in determining their own environmental the future. Repurpose global works with local businesses and social enterprises to provide sustainable alternatives to single-use plastics and to promote circular economy ideas, reducing plastic waste and preventing it from ending up in landfills or the ocean. This not only helps to save the environment, but it also accelerates economic development and social transformation in countries that need it the most.

Follow this link to learn more about them: rePurpose Global: World's Leading Plastic Action Platform

Plastic Pollution Coalition : Fighting for a plastic-free future

The Plastic Pollution Coalition is a non-profit communications and advocacy organization dedicated to combating and raising awareness about the negative impacts of plastic waste on our planet. The alliance, launched in 2009, brings together people, businesses, and organisations from a variety of industries to address the severe environmental disaster caused by plastic rubbish. The primary purpose of the coalition is to eliminate single-use plastics and promote sustainable alternatives. Through its advocacy campaign "Plastic Free future" the group strives to inspire change at both the individual and institutional levels. The Plastic Pollution Coalition also emphasizes the importance of education and awareness. Through campaigns, events, and educational resources, they strive to inform and inspire individuals to make conscious choices to reduce their plastic consumption. They encourage people to act in their daily lives, urging them to refuse single-use plastics, recycle responsibly, and support policies that prioritize plastic waste reduction. Overall, the Plastic Pollution Coalition is a leading force in the global fight against plastic pollution, working to create a healthier and more sustainable planet for future generations.

Follow this link to learn more about them: Plastic Pollution Coalition

Plastics for Change India foundation : Empowering communities to combat plastic pollution

Plastics for Change is a pioneering organisation dedicated to addressing the global challenge of plastic pollution. Plastics for Change has a novel approach to empower underprivileged waste pickers in India. They engage directly with waste pickers, recognising their vital role in the waste management ecosystem and providing them with fair and dignified employment options. The establishment of the Plastics for Change Marketplace is one of their significant accomplishments. This one-of-a-kind internet network connects waste pickers with socially and environmentally conscious firms, allowing them to sell recycled plastic that has been responsibly sourced. The organisation helps to reduce plastic waste in landfills and the environment by building a sustainable market for recovered plastic. Plastics for Change strives to improve the livelihoods of waste pickers and their communities by providing fair wages, safe working conditions, and access to healthcare and education. They have engaged with multiple stakeholders to advocate for sustainable waste management practices and raised awareness about the environmental and social consequences of plastic pollution.

Follow this link to learn more about them: Plastics for Change India Foundation
