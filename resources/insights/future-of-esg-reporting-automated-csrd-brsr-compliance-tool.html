The Future of ESG Reporting Is Automated: How ecoPRISM Is Leading the Way

  • 15 May 2025
  • By Atul S
  • 5-minute read

The regulatory landscape surrounding ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) reporting is rapidly evolving. What began as a voluntary initiative is now transitioning into a strategic imperative, enforced by government legislation across the globe. Global frameworks such as the CSRD in the EU, the SEC Climate Rule in the U.S., BRSR in India, and the ISSB standards are redefining how companies disclose and report their sustainability performance. As a result, the complexity of ESG compliance is intensifying.

Navigating a Fragmented Global Framework Landscape

Companies today face a wide array of ESG frameworks to comply with, each with distinct scope, metrics, and jurisdictional requirements:

  • CSRD & ESRS (European Union): Mandates detailed, double materiality-based sustainability disclosures with assurance requirements.
  • SEC Climate Disclosure & SASB (United States): Requires disclosure of climate-related financial risks and Scope 1-3 emissions, along with sector-specific metrics.
  • BRSR & BRSR Core (India): Requires India's top 1000 listed companies to report against nine ESG principles, including supply chain ethics, emissions, and social equity.
  • ISSB (IFRS S1 & S2): Introduces a globally aligned baseline for sustainability-related financial information, built on the TCFD framework.

Other widely adopted frameworks include GRI, TCFD, CDP, and SFDR - each introducing reporting overlaps and complexity for multinational companies.

Why ESG Reporting Needs Automation

The traditional approach to ESG reporting primarily relies on data collection through spreadsheets, emails, and disconnected systems. Conventional methods are now becoming outdated to meet the demands of today's regulatory and stakeholder expectations. Frameworks like the EU's CSRD require detailed, auditable, and assured disclosures across multiple entities and geographies.

Manual methods introduce significant risks, including human error, data inconsistency, compliance gaps, and operational inefficiency.

Automation addresses the growing complexity of ESG reporting by creating a unified, intelligent system that streamlines the entire disclosure process.

  • Integrated data collection: Automatically pulls relevant information from core business systems, eliminating manual data entry and reducing errors.
  • AI Powered Analytics: The data is then processed through AI-powered analytics that not only ensure regulatory alignment but also support scenario modelling, risk forecasting, and peer benchmarking, enabling sustainability teams to shift from reactive reporting to proactive planning.
  • Workflow automation: Connects cross-functional teams - legal, finance, sustainability, procurement—within a single platform. Tasks are assigned, progress is tracked, and accountability is maintained at every stage.

Manual reporting is no longer sustainable. The future is automated - and ecoPRISM is leading the way.
