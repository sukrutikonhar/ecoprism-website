Navigating CSRD - Best Practices for 2024

17th April, 2024
11 AM (GMT) | 12 PM CET

The Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) has become a crucial framework for organizations aiming to enhance their sustainability practices. During this hour-long session, our experts will guide you through crucial steps, outline data accuracy requirements, and share best practices for achieving compliance.

Webinar Highlights

  • Gain valuable insights from industry experts as they demystify CSRD requirements, providing a comprehensive understanding of the compliance landscape.
  • Walk away with clear objectives and the necessary tools to develop an effective plan for the 2024 fiscal year.
  • Recognize the significance of data accuracy and learn strategies to enhance the collection of high-quality, verifiable sustainability data.

Agenda

  • Introduction to CSRD: Understanding the Framework
  • Key Updates for 2024: What Organizations Need to Know
  • Challenges & Opportunities in CSRD Compliance
  • Best Practices for Effective Sustainability Reporting
  • Expert Panel Discussion:
    • Strategies for Streamlining CSRD Reporting
    • Data Management and Verification Techniques
    • Investor and Stakeholder Engagement
  • Q&A Session: Your Chance to Engage with the Experts
