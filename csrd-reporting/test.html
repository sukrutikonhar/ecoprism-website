From 2025, the Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) applies to large companies that meet at least two out of three of the following criteria:

250 or more employees

€40 million or more in net turnover

€20 million or more in total assets

If you answered "Yes" to at least two, you must report on your 2025 sustainability data in 2026.

The question is: Are you ready?
