ecoPRISM at Reuters IMPACT 2023

  • 20 Sep 2023
  • London, UK
  • 2-minute read

We are thrilled to share our recent participation as proud sponsors of the Reuters Sustainability Reporting Europe event (SREU), a prestigious gathering of industry leaders and innovators in the sustainability sector. Held on the 6th and 7th of September in the dynamic city of London, this event provided an exceptional platform for us to underscore our dedication to aid companies prepare for ESG reporting and transformation, in line with the upcoming regulations.

The main agenda of the Sustainability Reporting Stage, focused on the critical theme of "Collect, Manage, and Disclose Data Fit for the CSRD" (Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive). This directive is set to come into effect in less than six months for over 50,000 European companies. However, with nearly half of them not on track to comply, businesses are in a race against time to prepare. For a significant number of companies, a comprehensive revamping of their current systems and procedures is imperative to generate data that is both consistent, comparable, and of the requisite quality for regulatory compliance. Speed is of the essence, as businesses risk public declaration of their infraction and financial penalties if they fail to meet these new standards.

SREU brought together a distinguished roster of sustainability trailblazers, including corporates, industry experts, founders, and thought leaders, who engaged in enlightening discussions and shared their visions for a more sustainable future.

By sponsoring the SREU event, we were able to harmonize our core values with the event's overarching objectives, forging meaningful connections and contributing to the conversations that are shaping the sustainability landscape. Throughout the event, our team engaged in insightful discussions with esteemed thought leaders and industry professionals who share our fervor for sustainability. We were genuinely inspired by the wealth of knowledge and innovative dialogues that took place, and we eagerly anticipate infusing this newfound inspiration into our endeavors at ecoPRISM.

A notable highlight of our participation was the opportunity to showcase our comprehensive ESG platform to a receptive audience. The positive feedback we received from attendees affirmed the relevance and impact of our solutions for the corporates.

At ecoPRISM, we firmly believe that collaboration and the exchange of knowledge are fundamental drivers of progress in our industry. Our sponsorship and active involvement at SREU further solidify our commitment to fostering these collaborations and advancing the dialogue within the ESG domain.

Stay tuned for more updates on how we continue to make a difference. We look forward to future opportunities for collaboration and growth as we collectively steer the course toward a more sustainable future.
