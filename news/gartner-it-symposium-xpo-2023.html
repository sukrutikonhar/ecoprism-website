ecoPRISM Introduces a Sustainable Blueprint for Businesses at Gartner IT Symposium XPO: Guiding the Journey to ESG Excellence

  • 21 Nov 2023
  • Barcelona, Spain
  • 2-minute read

In a significant stride towards promoting Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) reporting and sustainability initiatives, ecoPRISM proudly participated as a platinum sponsor at the esteemed Gartner IT Symposium XPO. The event with over 7500 attendees provided a unique platform for industry leaders, tech enthusiasts, and decision-makers to converge and explore the transformative potential of technology.

As a leading proponent of sustainable business practices, ecoPRISM seized the opportunity to raise awareness about the pivotal role of ESG in today's corporate landscape. With the world witnessing a paradigm shift towards sustainability, the company highlighted the importance of aligning business strategies with ethical, environmental, and social considerations.

One of the focal points of ecoPRISM's engagement at the symposium was consultation sessions with decision-makers from diverse industries. The company's experts engaged in insightful discussions, understanding the unique needs and challenges faced by businesses in the context of ESG compliance.

"We believe that sustainable business practices are not only a moral imperative but also a strategic advantage in today's competitive landscape. By offering guidance and solutions for ESG reporting, we empower organizations to navigate the complexities of evolving regulations while contributing to a more sustainable future,"

Nagendra Siddoutam, CEO at ecoPRISM

An integral part of ecoPRISM's presence at the symposium was the expert speaker session featuring Stephane Crampel, Vice Président IS/IT EMEA at Camfil. An industry veteran with a wealth of experience, Stephane shared invaluable insights on leveraging technology for ESG compliance and transformation. His session delved into the practical aspects of integrating technology into sustainability initiatives, offering a roadmap for organizations seeking to enhance their ESG performance.

Stephane Crampel expressed optimism about the role of technology in driving positive change, stating, "In today's dynamic business environment, technology serves as a catalyst for ESG transformation. By adopting innovative solutions, organizations can not only meet compliance requirements but also foster a culture of sustainability that extends across their entire value chain."

The symposium provided a platform for attendees to gain a deeper understanding of the challenges and opportunities associated with ESG, fostering a collaborative environment for knowledge exchange and networking. ecoPRISM's platinum sponsorship exemplifies the company's commitment to being at the forefront of sustainable technology solutions and driving positive change in the corporate world.

As the business landscape continues to evolve, ecoPRISM remains dedicated to championing ESG reporting and sustainable roadmapping, offering organizations the tools and insights needed to thrive in an era where environmental and social responsibility are integral to success.
