Senior ESG Solutions Specialist

  • Full time
  • Bengaluru, IN
  • Sustainability

Job Description

At ecoPRISM, we're developing a comprehensive solution tailored for enterprises seeking enhanced management of their ESG data and performance. We aim to address corporate ESG challenges by automating data collection, generating advanced insights, and enabling scenario simulations to derive actionable strategies. Our SaaS-based platform empowers businesses to set and monitor ambitious targets while meeting diverse regulatory requirements, including adherence to the new CSRD regulations. ecoPRISM endeavors to furnish business leaders with the requisite tools and expertise essential to navigate their ESG transformation successfully.

You will be a key part of our Sustainability Team helping to fulfill our mission to enable a sustainable future with the right tools and technology.

Responsibilities

  • Support the product team with the scoping and design of the GHG accounting, ESG reporting and transformation within the ecoPRISM platform.
  • Apply extensive knowledge of GHG Protocol, International and European standards and frameworks like GRI, CSRD, EU Taxonomy, SFDR, CSDD, and others to ensure accuracy and effectiveness in compliance.
  • Tailor solutions to address the unique challenges and requirements of corporate clients and various industries.
  • Engage directly with clients to assist in analyzing their needs, collecting necessary data, and implementing the platform tailored to their specifications.
  • Stay abreast of evolving industry trends, ensuring the product remains relevant and compliant with the latest standards. Collaborate closely with cross-functional teams, spanning engineering, design, marketing, and sales, to seamlessly integrate products into customers' workflows.

Requirements

  • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in environmental science, Sustainability, or a related field.
  • Minimum 5 years of hands-on experience in ESG and sustainability regulations (EU Taxonomy, CSRD, SFDR, TCFD, ISSB, etc.), environmental and sustainability policies, and GHG standards, with at least 2 years in a consulting capacity.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with attention to detail.
  • Excellent communication (written and verbal) and presentation skills.
  • Ability to work collaboratively in a cross-functional team environment.

What you can expect from us

  • Compensation package tailored to your skills and experience.
  • Regular team-building events.
  • A team that values diversity and inclusion
  • An entrepreneurial, international, and dynamic work environment
  • A flexible and hybrid work environment.
  • A fast-growing company with a sense of purpose
