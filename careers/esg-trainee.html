ESG Trainee

  • Hybrid
  • Bengaluru, IN
  • Sustainability

Job Description

At ecoPRISM, we're developing a comprehensive solution tailored for enterprises seeking enhanced management of their ESG data and performance. Our platform addresses corporate ESG challenges by automating data collection, generating advanced insights, and enabling scenario simulations to derive actionable strategies. ecoPRISM empowers businesses to set and monitor ambitious targets while meeting evolving regulatory requirements such as the CSRD.

As a Trainee in the Sustainability Team, you’ll gain hands-on experience in ESG and sustainability while supporting the development and implementation of innovative solutions. This role is ideal for individuals passionate about sustainability and looking to kickstart their careers at the intersection of tech and ESG.

Responsibilities

  • Assist the product team in research and development for GHG accounting, ESG reporting, and sustainability transformation features in the ecoPRISM platform.
  • Support the integration of global ESG frameworks (e.g., GRI, CSRD, EU Taxonomy, SFDR) under guidance from senior team members.
  • Conduct market and policy research to help ensure product alignment with current regulations and best practices.
  • Work with internal teams to help analyze needs and contribute to ESG data collection and analysis efforts.
  • Stay updated on key ESG and sustainability developments, contributing to knowledge sharing across the team
  • Collaborate cross-functionally with product, engineering, and design teams.

Requirements

  • A recent graduate or final-year student pursuing a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Environmental Science, Sustainability, or a related discipline.
  • A basic understanding or strong interest in ESG topics, frameworks, and sustainability regulations.
  • Strong research, analytical, and problem-solving skills.
  • Excellent verbal and written communication.
  • Eagerness to learn, collaborate, and contribute in a fast-paced environment.

Training Program Details

  • Duration: 6 months
  • Confirmation: Based on performance, trainees may be offered a full-time role upon successful completion of the program

What you can expect from us

  • Mentorship and hands-on learning opportunities in a fast-growing ESG tech company.
  • A hybrid, flexible work environment
  • Exposure to cutting-edge ESG solutions and industry leaders
  • A collaborative, diverse, and inclusive team culture
  • Opportunities for career growth and full-time employment
