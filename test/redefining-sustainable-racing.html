Accelerating Sustainability: Revving Up Electric Racing with Modcrew and ecoPRISM

  • 17 May 2023
  • By Valli Tirounavoucarassou

We are excited to announce a collaboration with Modcrew, a premier motorsports organisation, to support and promote sustainable racing using the power of electric cars. This collaboration was on display during the recently hosted Hyderabad Speed Fest (Round-2 ELITE CUP) on May 13th and 14th, when electric racing took the spotlight.

As the world recognises the critical need of addressing climate change, supporting sustainable practises in all industries, including racing, has become critical. According to Forbes, driving an electric automobile is better for the environment than driving a traditional petrol car in 95% of the world. This notion also applies in the realm of race cars.

By collaborating with Modcrew, we hope to promote the environmental benefits and enormous potential of electric racing. Electric vehicles provide a cleaner and more sustainable alternative to gasoline-powered vehicles, with zero exhaust emissions and lower carbon footprints. As the world's energy systems move to renewable sources, the benefits of electric vehicles in racing will only continue to grow.

The partnership between ecoPRISM and Modcrew at the Hyderabad Speed Fest not only highlighted the exciting world of sustainable racing but also highlighted the organizations' dedication to making sustainability a reality for all stakeholders across various industries. Associations like this will play a crucial role in that drive positive change and inspiring others to adopt environmentally friendly solutions. ecoPRISM and Modcrew are happy to be at the forefront of this movement, bringing together innovation, enthusiasm, and the force of electric racing to pave the way for a greener, more sustainable future.
