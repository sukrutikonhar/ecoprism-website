Our Platform

Advancing Enterprise Sustainability

ecoPRISM is an all-in-one platform for automating sustainability data aggregation and using it for reporting and transformation purposes. Our platform seamlessly collects data from internal systems such as ERP and PLM, as well as external sources such as suppliers by utilizing pre-built data connectors and suite of portals and apps.

  • Collect And Aggregate ESG Data
  • Visualize And Explore Data Using Interactive Charts
  • Run Analyses And Create Data-Rich Reports
  • Identify Cost Impact of Your Sustainability Initiatives
