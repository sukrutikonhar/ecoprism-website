AI capabilities

Six ways ecoPRISM AI works for your team

01 Most distinctive

01 · AI-assisted ESG benchmarking

Know exactly where you stand -
globally.

Most benchmarking relies on self-reported data from a limited peer set. ecoPRISM's AI extracts and analyses publicly available ESG data from companies across the world - covering emissions, reporting frameworks, targets, materiality topics, and CSRD and BRSR disclosures.

  • Emissions data extracted from public disclosures
  • Framework coverage: CSRD, BRSR, GRI, TCFD
  • Targets set and progress tracked
  • Material topics by sector and region
  • Updated as new reports are published
02 Most distinctive

02 · AI-assisted report generation

Qualitative reporting -
without the manual ordeal.

The quantitative sections of an ESG report can be automated. The qualitative sections - governance narratives, policy descriptions, strategy explanations - are where ESG teams spend weeks.

  • Ingests your policies, reports, and data books
  • Prepares qualitative CSRD and GRI responses
  • Framework-aligned drafts ready for review
  • Reduces weeks of manual writing to hours
03 Most distinctive

03 · CSRD ecobot

Your CSRD expert -
available instantly.

CSRD and ESRS are complex. The nuances between disclosure requirements, materiality thresholds, and reporting boundaries take months to understand - and the regulation keeps evolving.

  • Trained on full CSRD regulation and all ESRS standards
  • Answers nuanced disclosure questions instantly
  • Available to your whole team via chat
  • Updated as regulation evolves
04

04 · Automated data collection

Your data, connected -
not manually uploaded.

ecoPRISM connects directly to the systems where your sustainability data already lives - ERP, HSE, BMS, and more - pulling structured data automatically without manual intervention.

  • Direct integrations with ERP systems
  • HSE and BMS data pulled automatically
  • AI reads and extracts data from PDFs
  • Real-time data flow with no manual uploads
05

05 · AI-based anomaly detection

Catch data issues before your auditor does.

ESG data errors are common - and in a world of mandatory third-party assurance, they are costly. ecoPRISM's anomaly detection learns from your historical emissions and ESG data patterns, and flags anything that deviates unexpectedly.

  • Learns from your historical data patterns
  • Flags unexpected spikes or inconsistencies
  • Catches errors before assurance review
  • Improves audit readiness automatically
06

06 · AI-powered recommendations

From data to action -
automatically.

Knowing your ESG performance is one thing. Knowing what to do about it is another. ecoPRISM's recommendation engine analyses your current performance data and surfaces specific, prioritised actions to improve your sustainability outcomes.

  • Based on your actual ESG performance data
  • Prioritised by impact and feasibility
  • Aligned to your targets and reporting frameworks
  • Updated as your data changes
